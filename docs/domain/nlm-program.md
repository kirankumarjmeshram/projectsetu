# National Livestock Mission program definitions

## Implemented rule version

ProjectSetu identifies the current composite rule version as
`2026-02-25-AMENDED-IMPLEMENTATION-GUIDELINES`. The Department of Animal
Husbandry and Dairying's order `R-99014/15/2023-Anlm_Dadf`, circulated on
25 February 2026, is an amendment to the existing guidelines rather than a
complete restatement. The implementation therefore reads the January 2025
comprehensive operational guideline as the base and overlays the provisions
changed by the February 2026 amendment.

The amendment expands entrepreneurship to indigenous horse, camel, donkey and
mule activities and broadens feed/fodder and innovation provisions. ProjectSetu
implements horse, donkey and camel because current official unit/cap evidence is
available. Mule entrepreneurship remains a manual-review boundary because a
current authoritative unit/cap table was not located.

No `effectiveTo` is fabricated.

## Independent program IDs

- `GOI.NLM.RURAL_POULTRY`
- `GOI.NLM.SHEEP_GOAT`
- `GOI.NLM.PIGGERY`
- `GOI.NLM.FEED_FODDER`
- `GOI.NLM.FODDER_SEED_PROCESSING`
- `GOI.NLM.HORSE`
- `GOI.NLM.DONKEY`
- `GOI.NLM.CAMEL`

Every activity uses 50% of verified eligible capital cost, subject to its own
unit-size or activity cap. There is deliberately no family-wide Rs 50 lakh cap.
Examples include Rs 25 lakh for the 1000+100 rural-poultry unit, Rs 10-50 lakh
for configured sheep/goat sizes, Rs 15/30 lakh for configured piggery sizes,
and the separate camel unit matrix.

## Costs and finance

Activity-specific capital items are eligible. Express exclusions are land
purchase, land rent/lease, working capital, personal vehicles and office
accommodation. Every input cost remains in the result with its eligible and
excluded amount; the evaluator never deletes bankable-project costs.

The remainder after calculated subsidy may be financed through a bank or
financial institution or through source-backed self-finance. Bank/financial
institution mode and self-finance mode have different conditions. Self-finance
requires bank appraisal, expenditure/verification evidence and the prescribed
three-year bank guarantee for the cost beyond subsidy.

The subsidy result is eligibility only. The two 50% instalments retain their
first-disbursement or 25%-expenditure trigger and completion/State Implementing
Agency verification trigger. Sanction and receipt are not simulated.

## Manual-review boundaries

Unconfigured animal sizes, unknown cost components, missing unit evidence,
missing finance mode and missing activity/entity facts never default to an
eligible result. The module does not implement non-entrepreneurship NLM
sub-missions, sanction workflow, actual SIDBI release, mule unit economics or a
cross-scheme funding stack.
