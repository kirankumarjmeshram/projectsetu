# Maharashtra CMEGP program definition

## Current version and sources

`MH.CMEGP.NEW_ENTERPRISE` uses version `2025-05-21-AMENDED-GR`. The governing
source is Maharashtra GR `MuroNi-2024/Pra.Kra.98/Industries-7`, issued on
21 May 2025 with amended provisions effective from 1 April 2025. The original
1 August 2019 GR remains the base only for provisions retained by the amendment.
The 28 October 2025 verification GR confirms current online operation and the
revised asset-verification/adjustment process. No `effectiveTo` is invented.

## Jurisdiction, ceilings and margin money

The project location must explicitly be Maharashtra. The current project
ceilings are Rs 1 crore for manufacturing and Rs 50 lakh for service and
agri-allied projects.

| Beneficiary | Area  | Service/agri rate (cap) | Manufacturing rate (cap) |
| ----------- | ----- | ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| General     | Urban | 15% (Rs 3 lakh)         | 15% (Rs 7.5 lakh)        |
| General     | Rural | 25% (Rs 5 lakh)         | 25% (Rs 12.5 lakh)       |
| Special     | Urban | 25% (Rs 5 lakh)         | 25% (Rs 12.5 lakh)       |
| Special     | Rural | 35% (Rs 7 lakh)         | 35% (Rs 17.5 lakh)       |

The retained beneficiary contribution is 10% for general and 5% for special
category. Expected bank finance is the admissible project cost less the required
contribution and calculated margin money. This is a source-specific CMEGP
matrix; it is not copied from PMEGP.

The 2025 amendment verifies working-capital limits of 40% of project cost for
manufacturing and 60% for service/agri-allied activities. Fixed building and
machinery/equipment costs are modeled. The current GR material reviewed did not
provide sufficiently precise current treatment for land, rental/lease, vehicles
or arbitrary other costs, so those inputs remain visible, are excluded from the
calculation basis and produce manual review rather than guessed eligibility.

The amended activity list includes manufacturing, service, agri/agri-allied,
e-mobility, branded chain and mobile outlets, poultry/hatchery, beekeeping,
fisheries, sericulture, hotel/dhaba, homestay, cloud kitchen and specified
water/passenger-boat activities. The State-level committee can publish a
negative list; an unverified activity therefore does not default to eligible.

## Release boundary

Margin money is credit-linked and held against the financing loan account; it is
not immediate beneficiary cash. Physical asset verification becomes due after
two years from first loan disbursement. Adjustment occurs only after three years
and District Level Task Force Committee approval. The module records those
conditions but never fabricates sanction, release, receipt or adjustment.
